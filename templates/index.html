Frequently Asked Questions

Below you'll find answers to the most common questions about our three main services.

The AI Resume Chatbot is an interactive service that uses artificial intelligence to help you build a professional resume in real-time. Simply provide your personal and professional details, and the chatbot will generate a complete CV for you, including summary, education, work experience, skills, and more.

To access the chatbot, click on the "Start Now" button under the AI Resume Chatbot service. Then, simply type in your information or respond to the chatbot's prompts. Once your resume is generated, you can download it as a DOCX file for further editing or sharing.

The CV Classification service analyzes the text of your resume and classifies it into a specific category, such as Python Developer, Data Scientist, Java Developer, and more. This helps recruiters and hiring managers quickly understand your professional focus and expertise.

To use this feature, navigate to the CV Classification page and paste your resume text into the provided field. Then, click on the "Start Now" button. The system will process your resume and display the most likely job category based on your skills and experience.

CV Analysis & Rating evaluates your resume based on various criteria like skills, experience, education, and overall relevance. It provides a numerical score along with suggestions on how to enhance your resume and identify the most suitable job title.

Simply go to the CV Analysis & Rating page and upload your resume in PDF format. Once uploaded, the system will process your resume and provide a detailed score along with improvement recommendations. It also suggests a potential job role that matches your background.
